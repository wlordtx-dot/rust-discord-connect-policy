# Privacy Policy **Last Updated: October 5, 2026** ## 1. Information We Collect The Rust Discord Connect Bot may process the following information: * Discord User ID * SteamID * Account-linking status * Temporary verification codes * Authentication and technical logs ## 2. How We Use Information Information is used only to: * Link Rust and Discord accounts * Verify account ownership through the linking process * Maintain server security * Troubleshoot technical issues * Prevent misuse of the service ## 3. Data Storage Account-linking records may be stored by the Rust server and its plugins to maintain the linking functionality. Temporary verification codes are intended only for account authentication. ## 4. Data Sharing We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by Discord, Steam, and the hosting infrastructure as necessary for the service to operate. ## 5. Data Retention and Deletion Users may contact the Rust server administration team to request assistance with unlinking accounts or deleting eligible account-linking records. Some technical records may be retained when necessary for security or operational purposes. ## 6. Security Reasonable measures are used to protect account-linking information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. ## 7. Third-Party Services The Bot may rely on Discord, Steam, uMod, and server hosting services. Their respective privacy policies may also apply. ## 8. Contact For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact the Rust server administration team through its official Discord server.
